# GeoAR

**GeoAR** is an augmented reality geography app that makes learning about India’s terrain and culture more immersive for students.

## Features

- **AR Map of India**: Uses image targets to overlay 3D terrain, rivers, landmarks, and cultural points on a physical map.
- **Interactive Overlays**: Tap-based interactions to reveal facts, animations, and region-specific information.
- **Student-Friendly UX**: Designed for quick onboarding and intuitive gestures suited to school environments.
- **Hackathon-Ready Demo**: Built and polished for live demos in competitive hackathon settings.

## Tech Stack

- Unity (core engine, scene management, interactions)
- Vuforia (image tracking and AR targets)
- Android (mobile build and deployment)
